      \begin{tablenotes}\item \textit{Notes:} The dependent variable is the log change between consecutive month-ends of a firm's position at a bank. For credit lines (columns 1 and 3) it is the total commitment, drawn credit (\textit{cr\'edito vigente}) plus the unused line (\textit{l\'inea no utilizada}); for term loans (columns 2 and 4) it is drawn credit. Credit is classified with a manual product table: credit cards, current-account advances, guarantees, letters of credit, credits to be settled, multi-line facilities and other lines are credit lines; leasing, lease-back, real-estate, mortgage, pawn, income-property and RFA credit are term loans; \textit{pr\'estamos} are credit lines when reported as revolving, term loans when non-revolving, and, when the revolving status is not identified, credit lines if the row carries an unused line and term loans otherwise.
