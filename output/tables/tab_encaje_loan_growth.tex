      \begin{tablenotes}\item \textit{Notes:} The dependent variable is the log change between consecutive month-ends of a firm's position at a lender. For credit lines (columns 1 and 3) it is the total commitment, drawn credit (\textit{cr\'edito vigente}) plus the unused line (\textit{l\'inea no utilizada}); for term loans (columns 2 and 4) it is drawn credit. Lenders are all institutions with a reserve shock: commercial banks, cajas municipales and rurales, and financieras.
